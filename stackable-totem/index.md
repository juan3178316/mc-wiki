<h1 style="text-align: left;"><span style="color: rgb(230, 126, 35);"><strong>How works</strong></span></h1>

## <span style="color: rgb(53, 152, 219);"><strong>Stacking</strong></span>

<div class="spoiler"><p style="text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juan3178316/mc-wiki/refs/heads/main/media/gif/stackable_totem_stacking_1_0_0-15_05_26-0.22.gif" alt="" width="440" height="196"></p><p style="text-align: left;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left;"><span style="color: rgb(45, 194, 107);"><em>only interact anywhere with a totem of undying, select "<strong>stack more totems" and you can stack all totems in your inventory <span style="color: rgb(241, 196, 15);">(No detect totems in your offhand)</span>.</strong></em></span></p></div>

<div class="spoiler"><p style="text-align: center;"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/juan3178316/mc-wiki/refs/heads/main/media/gif/stackable_totem_unstacking_1_0_0-15_05_26-0.21.gif" alt="Unstacking totem" width="448" height="200"></p><p style="text-align: left;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left;"><span style="color: rgb(241, 196, 15);"><em>Only interact anywhere, select <strong>"Unstack totem</strong>", after you choose what amount you want Unstack and spawn a totem ahead you.&nbsp;<span style="color: rgb(224, 62, 45);">(</span></em><strong><em><span style="color: rgb(224, 62, 45);">Please, no make this action in top of the dimension, or you lost your totem unstacked).</span></em></strong></span></p></div>
